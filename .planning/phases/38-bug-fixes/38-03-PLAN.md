  5. The field should be: `demographicsError: 'token_expired' | 'api_error' | null`

  Important: Do NOT change the overall response structure or break existing consumers. Just ADD the `demographicsError` field. Keep the existing behavior where `aggregatedDemographics` is null/undefined when demographics fail -- the error field is supplementary context.
  </action>
